# 1. What is System Design?

[🏠 Index](../README.md) · [2. How to Approach System Design? →](../basics/02-how-to-approach-system-design.md)

---

**Set of requirements** →
- decide architecture
- decide components
- decide modules

…and **how they interact with each other** → *to solve the problem*.

System design is *almost* "Product Development".

### Why is it so popular?
- Every single "tech" product is a "system" that has been "designed".
- Companies are building products and need **you all** to **design** it.

### Why is understanding system design important?
- This is what people do at work.
- Everything is practical.
- You can see it from Day 1 of joining the company.
- Once you grow in your career, you will spend **80% of time** doing this.

**System design is hence relevant for literally everyone!!**

### Side effects of System Design
- It will make everything else uninteresting 🙂
- You solve **real** problems, not something made up.
- Break down problem statement.
- Re-wires your brain to think in a structured way
  - ↳ considering all possible cases to deliver a **great user experience!**

### What will we do when we design a system?
- Break down problem statement into **solvable** sub-problems
- Decide on key components and responsibilities
- Decide on boundaries of each component so each do unique work and we reuse components
- Touch upon key challenges in scaling it
- Make our architecture fault-tolerant and available
